Posted 10/05/2023

MEMORANDUM FOR ALL ELECTRONIC POINT OF SALES (EPoS) DISPATCH USERS.

SUBJECT: FuelsManager® Defense (FMD) Enterprise Dispatch Function Update

Following the deployment of the Electronic Point of Sale (EPoS), several Defense Fuel Support Points (DFSPs) are still conducting Dispatch Operations in both FMD and EPoS Dispatch. In accordance with the DLA Energy EPoS Directive Type Memorandum (DTM), effective 3/1/2023, it requires that DFSPs convert to EPoS Dispatch functionality.

To support this requirement, DLA Energy Information Operations (J6) and Energy established a new FMD Dispatch role permission set that removed users’ ability to create transactions within the module, but still allow the remaining FMD dispatch functionality after EPoS deployment. If there are extenuating circumstances that will not allow a location to process dispatch transactions in EPoS, the DFSP must follow the waiver process per DLA Energy EPoS DTM (Enclosure 1, Para 3a).

DLA Energy has established procedures to convert current FMD Dispatch users to the new role permission set 30 days from this message date for locations that already have EPoS deployed with no actions required by the DFSP personnel. Locations scheduled to receive EPoS in the future will have 30 days after the deployment completion date to transition to EPoS Dispatch unless an approved waiver is on file. Recommended personnel should complete the steps below to better facilitate the transition.
  1. Establish a Fuels Training Portal account - FuelsTraining
  2. Review the EPoS Dispatch video and other related training material in the Fuels Training Portal
  3. Review EPoS Enterprise User Guide: Section 5-Dispatch Feature

If you have any additional questions, please send your emails to the DLA Energy Business Process Support Directorate, System Sustainment Team at energy.asg@dla.mil.
